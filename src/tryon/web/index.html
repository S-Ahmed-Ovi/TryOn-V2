    How is the match calculated?

    85% shape harmony. How well the frame's shape suits your face shape, using classic fitting rules. If your face sits between two shapes, we blend both.

    15% proportions. How well the frame's depth suits the length of your face. This part is a rule of thumb, so it only nudges the ranking.

    These are style guidelines, not strict rules. The best frame is one you love wearing.

    How it works

    From selfie to short-list in three moves.

    1. 1

      Show us your face

      Upload a photo or take one here. It's read in memory and discarded straight after. We never store it.

    2. 2

      We measure it

      478 points on your face give us its length, cheekbones, jaw and forehead, which together decide your face shape.

    3. 3

      See your matches

      Every frame gets a match score and a reason. Tap one to see it on you, in a quick 2D view or as a real 3D model, then nudge, resize and save the look.

    Face shape guide

    The idea is simple: contrast and balance.

    Angular frames sharpen soft faces. Curved frames soften strong ones. Wide frames balance long faces. Choose a shape to see its best frames.
